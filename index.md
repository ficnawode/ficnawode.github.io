Recently escaped a physics degree.

My free time tends to go toward inventing and reinventing the wheel. Especially very fundamental wheels: compilers, linkers, languages in general, emulators, firmware, basic electronics, 3D printing, machining, whatever tech happens to land in my hands.

Custom keyboards are another recurring rabbit hole, despite the ongoing trouble of actually getting used to them. Recently made a handwired, wireless Miryoku Skeletyl that might get a write-up soon. It's kind of like mushrooms: fun to run around the forest picking them, but no fun to eat.

I spend my working hours somewhere in the physical layer of the cellular stack, writing lots of C.

Not an AI doomer, not an accelerationist either. As a great warrior-poet once put it:

```
You can just do things.
```

Seems like a good way to go about it.
